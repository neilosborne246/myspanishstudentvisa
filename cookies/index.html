Cookie Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026  ·  My Spanish Student Visa / Retail Consulting UK Ltd

This cookie policy explains what cookies are, which cookies we use on myspanishstudentvisa.com, and how you can manage your preferences. For broader information on how we handle your personal data, please read our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the website to remember your actions and preferences over a period of time, so you don’t have to keep re-entering them when you return or browse between pages. Cookies can be first-party (set by us) or third-party (set by external services). They may be session cookies (deleted when you close your browser) or persistent cookies (stored for a set period).

2. How We Use Cookies

We use one first-party cookie to remember your cookie choice. With your consent, we also use Google Analytics cookies to understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it. We do not use advertising or marketing cookies.

3. Cookies We Use

3.1 Strictly Necessary Cookie

This cookie is set when you make a choice in our cookie banner, so we do not ask you again on every page. It contains only the word “granted” or “denied” and no personal information.

4. Cookies We Do Not Use

My Spanish Student Visa does not use:

  • Advertising, marketing or retargeting cookies (such as the Meta Pixel or Google Ads)
  • Session recording or heatmap cookies (e.g. Hotjar or Microsoft Clarity)
  • Live chat widget cookies
  • Cookies set by embedded third-party social media widgets

Our online application dashboard runs on a separate website, which sets its own cookies and explains them there.

5. Your Cookie Choices

When you first visit myspanishstudentvisa.com, a cookie banner asks whether you accept analytics cookies. Accepting and rejecting are equally easy, and analytics stays off unless you accept.

You can change your choice at any time using the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of every page, or here: . If you withdraw consent, we delete the Google Analytics cookies from your browser.

Most browsers also let you block or delete cookies through their settings menus:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

You can also opt out of Google Analytics on every website by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this cookie policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

7. Contact

If you have questions about our use of cookies, please contact us at hola@myspanishvisa.com.
