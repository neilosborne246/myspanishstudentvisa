Health Insurance for Your Family
on a Spain Student Visa

If your partner or children are coming to Spain with you, each of you needs health insurance that meets the visa rules. Cover and eligibility are assessed for each person, so the student, a partner and each child may need different cover. Request cover for your family in one enquiry.

Each Person

Health Cover for Each Person in Your Family

Spain checks each applicant's insurance on its own. Spanish Health Insurance reviews the circumstances of each person. Available cover is subject to eligibility, any required underwriting and confirmation in the quote and policy documents.

The student

Student health insurance is designed for people studying in Spain, and it has its own eligibility rules, including age. If you are the student, your quote confirms which cover fits your age, course and stay.

Your partner

A spouse, registered partner or stable partner must have suitable cover in their own name that meets the visa criteria. It is not automatically the same product as the student's: your partner's age and situation are assessed on their own.

Your children

Each child must have suitable cover in their own name for the whole stay, including a partner's children who come with you. Children are assessed individually too. Provide each child's date of birth when requesting your family quote.

One enquiry for the household

You can request cover for everyone at once. Listing several people in one enquiry does not mean they all qualify for the same product. Available cover and eligibility are confirmed for each person with your personalised quote.

The Visa Rules

What Every Family Member's Policy Must Meet

The same health insurance rules apply to the student and to each family member (Royal Decree 1155/2024, arts. 35.i and 56, and the Ministry's criteria of 7 August 2025).

  • An insurer authorised to operate in Spain
  • Full cover, comparable to the Spanish public health system's common basic services
  • No co-payments, not reimbursement-only, and no waiting periods
  • If the policy caps its cover at a fixed amount, that cap must be at least €30,000
  • In force for the whole period of each person's authorisation

Some consulates list extra details in their own checklists, so check your consulate's checklist for each applicant. See what the insurance requirement covers and what “no co-payments” means in practice.

Who Can Come

Which Families Can Join a Student in Spain

Family members can accompany only students in higher education (university degrees, official master's and PhD programmes, the Máster de Formación Profesional) or specialised health training. They include a spouse, a registered partner or a proven stable partner, unmarried children under 18 of the student or their partner, and adult children who need specific support because of disability or illness (Royal Decree 1155/2024, art. 56).

The family visa rules, documents and money requirements are explained in our family visa guide, with details for partners, children and the documents each family member needs.

Request Cover

How to Request Health Cover for Your Family

What you will be asked

  • Each person's date of birth and relationship to the student
  • The province or city where you will live in Spain
  • When you want cover to start and how long you need it
  • Your contact details

What happens next

Spanish Health Insurance reviews each person's circumstances and confirms the cover available, subject to eligibility and any required underwriting. Your available policy duration and eligibility will be confirmed with your personalised quote.
