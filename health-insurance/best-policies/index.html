What the Visa Requires

What Spain Requires From Student Visa Health Insurance

Spain's immigration regulation (Real Decreto 1155/2024, article 35(i)) requires student visa applicants to hold health insurance, and the Ministry's criteria set out what that policy must look like.

The policy must

  • Be issued by an insurer authorised to operate in Spain
  • Give full cover comparable to the Spanish public system's common basic portfolio of services
  • Have no co-payments
  • Not be reimbursement-only
  • Have no waiting periods
  • Be valid for the whole of your stay

Points that often cause confusion

  • €30,000 minimum: the Ministry’s criteria require at least €30,000 where a policy caps its cover at a fixed amount. Some consulates, notably in the US (Boston, Chicago, New York, Los Angeles, San Francisco), also list it — check your own consulate's list.
  • No approved list: there is no official government list of insurers. The consulate checks the policy, not the brand.
  • Working students: a student working under article 57 and registered with Social Security meets the insurance requirement through that registration (article 57.5).
What to Check

What to Check in Any Student Health Insurance Policy

Use this checklist for any policy you are considering, whoever sells it. Read the policy conditions and the certificate, not just the sales summary.

Authorised insurer

The insurer must be authorised to operate in Spain. A home-country policy or a travel insurance product will not normally meet the requirement.

Full cover

Cover comparable to the Spanish public system's common basic portfolio: primary care, specialists, diagnostics, emergencies, hospitalisation and surgery.

No co-payments

No fee per visit, test or treatment. Check the policy conditions, not just the plan name — many policies are sold in versions with and without co-payments. What no copay means.

Not reimbursement-only

A policy that only pays you back after you have paid a doctor yourself does not meet the criteria.

No waiting periods

Cover must apply from the policy start date, with no waiting period (carencia) before you can use it.

Dates covering your stay

The policy must be valid for the whole stay you are applying for. Check the start date fits your arrival and the end date covers your course.

Certificate

You need an insurance certificate for your visa file. Check it shows your name, the policy dates, and confirms full cover without co-payments or waiting periods.

Exclusions

Every policy has exclusions. Sanitas International Students, for example, excludes traffic accidents, work accidents and risky sports, and anything directly associated with pregnancy, delivery and newborn care.

Age limits

Some student policies set an age limit for taking out cover. Sanitas International Students can be taken out from age 14 to 35.

Over 35?

Applicants over 35 need a different policy that still meets the visa criteria. See health insurance over 35.

Family members

Each partner or child joining you needs their own full-cover policy meeting the visa criteria. Do not assume a student product's terms apply to them. Health insurance for your family.

Where you will live

Premiums can depend on your province. Sanitas policies cannot currently be arranged for residents of Navarra or Cantabria.

Useful, but not required for the visa

  • English-speaking customer service
  • A good clinic and hospital network near your study city
  • Video consultation or an online doctor service
  • Dental cover
  • Mental health support
Other Spanish insurers: other Spanish insurers also sell student policies. We do not arrange, compare or quote other insurers' policies, so we cannot say whether a particular one is suitable. If you are considering one, check it against every point above and read its policy conditions and certificate before you buy.
