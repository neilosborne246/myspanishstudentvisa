Spain Student Visa 2026

Applying for a Spain student visa from Johannesburg, Pretoria or elsewhere in Gauteng in 2026? The Embassy of Spain in Pretoria is responsible for your application, and you hand it in by appointment at the BLS visa application centre in Pretoria. There is no Spanish consulate in Johannesburg.

At a Glance

Spain Student Visa — Embassy of Spain in Pretoria, South Africa

Two different places are involved. The Embassy of Spain in Pretoria is the office responsible for your application and decides it. BLS International runs the visa application centre where you hand in your application by appointment. Spain’s only office in Johannesburg is a commercial office, not a consulate. DIRCO in Pretoria handles the apostille — allow 2–4 weeks. Factor in courier time if outside Gauteng.

Documents

What to Bring: Embassy of Spain in Pretoria

Standard Documents (All Consulates)

  • Original passport + photocopy of data page
  • EX-00 form completed in Spanish and signed
  • Enrolment letter from your Spanish school
  • Bank statements (3–6 months)
  • Spain student visa-compliant health insurance
  • Medical certificate from a licensed doctor
  • Proof of accommodation in Spain
  • 2 passport-sized photographs

South African Documents

  • Criminal record: SAPS (South African Police Service) police clearance certificate
  • Apostille from: DIRCO (Department of International Relations and Cooperation) in Pretoria
  • Sworn Spanish translation of apostilled certificate
  • Proof that you live in the consular district
  • Visa fee payment when you submit
